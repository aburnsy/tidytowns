## Description

A formal heritage trail connecting the Norman castle, the old forge, the church, and the bog walk entrance would create a coherent visitor experience and showcase the village's rich history. A central map at the village green plus wayfinding signs tie the individual sites into a unified trail. It came up at committee as a great way to pull all our historical bits and pieces into one walk for visitors, rather than the sites just sitting on their own.
