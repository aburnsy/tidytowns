## Description

It came up at committee that we'd be a lot stronger with real survey numbers to point to year on year, a bird count, a pollinator count, maybe a wetland survey down by the bog walk. We can run them ourselves with the volunteer team, or rope in a local naturalist group for a hand. Beats vague claims that things are getting better.

## Best practice & research

The [All-Ireland Pollinator Plan - Monitoring](https://pollinators.ie/monitoring/) provides guidance on how to survey for pollinators in your local area, including standardised methods that allow results to be compared year on year. Community-level monitoring contributes valuable data to national biodiversity records.
