## Description

Installing heritage interpretation panels at the Norman castle, the old forge, the church, and the bog walk entrance would transform these sites into an engaging open-air history trail. Good panels tell the story of a place, deepen community pride, and give visitors a reason to stop and read. Came up at committee as a high-value next step once funding's in place.
