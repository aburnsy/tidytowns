A spot to stop and look back into the village. The seating area sits at the western end of Two Mile Borris, just before you come up to the Blackcastle, with bench seating tucked in behind clipped Box topiary and a Laurel hedge. The gravel path keeps the place feeling neat year-round and the wildflower planting either side does the heavy lifting for our pollinators.

In May the Alliums come up in big purple drumsticks alongside Dandelion clocks gone to seed, and there is a beautiful run of red roses right across the road. The whole spot is small but does a lot of work, both as a quiet bench for anyone wanting a sit-down and as a piece of the western approach into the village.
