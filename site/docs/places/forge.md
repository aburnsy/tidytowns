The Old Forge sits right in the middle of the main street, beside the parking for Corcoran's. You can spot it straight away by the iron star set into the wall above the archway, the ivy creeping up over the gable, and the lovely cast-iron bench planted out front. It is one of the small heritage features that makes the streetscape feel grounded.

Forges were once the engine room of every village in Ireland, ours included. The smith would have been shoeing horses, fixing carts, mending tools, and standing in for half a dozen modern trades all at once. The building has been kept in good order ever since the working forge closed, and we are proud to have it as a quiet anchor on the main street. There is a future project on the books to look at a proper interpretation panel here so visitors can read a bit of the story without having to ask.
