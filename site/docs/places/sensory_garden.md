The Sensory Garden sits right beside Scoil Mochaomhóg Naofa NS on the L4202, doubling as an outdoor classroom for the school and a quiet spot for anyone passing through. The whole space has been built up over the years with locally sourced timber, a lovely yellow-plinth pergola at the entrance, a drystone amphitheatre tucked in behind, and gravel paths winding through the lot. From the air you can see how nicely the whole thing sits in among the village, with Cherry blossom going mad on either side in spring.

The planting is built around the senses. Hawthorn and willow do most of the work for shade and scent, with carved wooden sculptures made from recycled tree stumps tucked in among the trees for the children to find. We are also weaving living willow features into the garden, a willow dome for shelter and play, and willow rods worked into the boundary planting alongside natural stone and reclaimed timber. Once the rods root and leaf out they make a brilliant natural feature, and the early catkins are great for the bees too.

The 2025 adjudicator flagged the timber paths for ongoing maintenance, and we keep an eye on them as part of the regular volunteer round. There is a separate completed project covering the willow reweaving work, and the sensory garden is one of the spots that turns up most often in our photos because there is always something on the go here.
