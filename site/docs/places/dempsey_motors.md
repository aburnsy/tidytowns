For decades the Dempsey & Harold Motors garage was a working part of the village streetscape. In 2026 the proprietor retired and the site has been taken over by Roadvacs (Irl) Limited, who are redeveloping it as their new Tipperary base. Roadvacs are a distributor of liquid-waste-equipment, so the new use will be offices and a yard rather than the trucking operation people sometimes assume from the name.

This is a really positive Streetscape change in the pipeline at this end of the village, and a direct response to the kind of derelict-or-underused frontage the 2025 adjudicator flagged on the main street. We have one of our blue trough planters at the spot already, planted up with red Geum, ornamental grass and a mix of perennials, so even with the redevelopment under way it stays welcoming. We are looking forward to seeing how the finished site lands.
