For decades the Dempsey & Harold Motors garage was a working part of the village streetscape. The proprietor retired in spring 2026 and the garage closed, only a month or so before this is being written, and that closure is a sad loss for the village in itself. A long-running family business stitched into the daily rhythm of the place is not something you replace lightly.

The site has been taken on by Roadvacs (Irl) Limited as their new Tipperary base. Roadvacs are a distributor of liquid-waste-equipment, so the new use will be offices and a yard rather than the trucking operation people sometimes assume from the name. We are excited by the prospect of new businesses and offices coming into a frontage that might otherwise have sat empty, and looking forward to seeing how the finished site lands.

We have one of our blue trough planters at the spot already, planted up with red Geum, ornamental grass and a mix of perennials, so even with the redevelopment under way it stays welcoming.
