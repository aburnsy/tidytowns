Corcoran's Bar and Shop is the third of the main-street pubs, with the bar entrance in the middle of the building and a separate shop entrance off to the right. From a streetscape point of view it is one of the more prominent fronts on the L4202, and on a bright winter day with St James Church framed at the end of the row, it is one of the best vantage points in the village.

Out front are some of the older concrete planters from the village stock. We have been bringing them into line with our cohesive village planter scheme by filling them with split perennials from our other beds, instead of buying annuals every year. No waste, no spend on plants we already grow, and a much better deal for our pollinators in the long run.
