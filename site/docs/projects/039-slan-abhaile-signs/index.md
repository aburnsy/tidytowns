## Description

Adding "Slán Abhaile" (Safe Home) to the reverse side of the village welcome signs is a small but characterful Irish-language touch that bids visitors farewell as they leave. Someone raised it at committee as a nice farewell for people leaving the village. Small, cheap, very us.
