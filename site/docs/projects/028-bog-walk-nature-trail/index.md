## Description

The Bog Walk Loop is one of TMB's biggest amenity assets but was completely absent from the 2025 application. Phase 1 is about getting the loop back to a usable state, concentrated on the Ballyduff side. Phase 2 (surfacing for accessibility) and Phase 3 (biodiversity interpretation) sit as separate projects and follow on once Phase 1 lands.

## What Phase 1 covers

- Clearing overhanging branches and cutting back dense overgrown scrub along the route
- Tidying the boundary hedges
- Laying a stone base course along the route to firm up the surface underfoot
- Installing removable bollards (or large boulders if removable bollards aren't viable) at the access points to stop vehicular access onto the loop

## Funding

Tidy Towns is paying up front, the largest single capital commitment we are making in 2026. A LEADER grant has been secured to cover the majority of that cost, with Cllr Sean Ryan instrumental in helping get it landed. Once the money is recouped it rolls straight into Phase 2.

## What comes next

- [**Phase 2 (2027)**](../029-bog-walk-surface-upgrade/index.md): properly surface the route for buggies, kids' bikes and less-confident walkers.
- [**Phase 3 (2029)**](../030-bog-walk-biodiversity-interpretation/index.md): biodiversity interpretation panels along the trail, then wildlife panels at a handful of village spots.

## Best practice & research

- [All-Ireland Pollinator Plan](https://pollinators.ie/), guidance on habitat management and species-friendly planting alongside the trail.
- [Leave No Trace Ireland](https://www.leavenotraceireland.org/), principles for managing a nature trail that protects the very habitat it showcases.
