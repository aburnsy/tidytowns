## Description

The Bog Walk Loop is one of TMB's biggest amenity assets but was completely absent from the 2025 application. This project transforms it from an unmentioned walking path into a fully featured biodiversity nature trail, restored as a usable village amenity first and then built up over time with proper surfacing and biodiversity interpretation.

## Phased delivery

**Phase 1: Clearing and access works on the Ballyduff side (2026, paid up front by Tidy Towns at roughly €10,000).** Clearing overhanging branches and cutting back dense overgrown scrub along the route; tidying the boundary hedges. Laying a stone base course along the route to firm up the surface underfoot. Installing removable bollards (or large boulders if removable bollards aren't viable) at the access points to ensure no vehicular access onto the loop. The Phase 1 works concentrate on the Ballyduff side of the loop. Tidy Towns is paying up front, the largest single capital commitment we are making in 2026. A large grant is expected to cover the majority of that cost, with Cllr Sean Ryan instrumental in helping get it secured. Once the money is recouped it rolls straight into Phase 2.

**Phase 2: Surface upgrade for accessibility (subsequent year).** Once Phase 1 lands and the recouped grant funds come back into the project, the next priority is properly surfacing the route so the loop is accessible to buggies, kids' bikes and less-confident walkers. Further grants will be pursued for any extra spend.

**Phase 3 (when capacity allows): Biodiversity interpretation along the trail.** Interpretation panels and species ID boards along the route. Then later, wildlife information panels at 3 to 4 additional key spots around the village. (The wider [heritage trail](../../future/025-heritage-trail/index.md) sitting in the Future pile would tie this in.)

## Best practice & research

- [All-Ireland Pollinator Plan](https://pollinators.ie/), guidance on habitat management, species-friendly planting alongside the trail, and how to design effective interpretation materials that help visitors identify and appreciate native species and habitats.
- [Leave No Trace Ireland](https://www.leavenotraceireland.org/), principles for managing a nature trail that protects the very habitat it showcases.
