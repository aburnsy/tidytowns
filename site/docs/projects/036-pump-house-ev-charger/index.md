## Description

The village pump house is expected to be **decommissioned in the near future**. Crucially, the site already has **three-phase power** running to it — exactly the supply needed for a fast EV charger. We propose to apply, when the time is right, to **convert the pump house site into a community EV charging point**.

This is a concrete, infrastructure-grade sustainability project in the same category that has historically scored well in the Tidy Towns Sustainability & Circular Economy award (precedents in other Tidy Towns entries — including a €15k SEAI grant for a similar community energy project — prove there is funded support out there for projects of this kind).

## Why this site

- **Three-phase electrical supply already in place** — fast-charger ready, no expensive grid upgrade required.
- **Pump house being retired** — no use conflict, the building/footprint becomes available rather than displacing existing infrastructure.
- **Visible, accessible location in the village** — usable by residents and visitors, supporting the wider transition to electric vehicles.

## Pre-requisites

1. **Confirm pump-house decommissioning timeline** with Irish Water / Tipperary County Council.
2. **Confirm legal site control** — who will own/manage the site post-decommissioning, and on what terms a community group could occupy it.
3. **Identify funding route** — SEAI Community Grant, ZEVI (Zero Emission Vehicles Ireland) public charging schemes, Tipperary County Council Climate Action funds. Apply once the site is available.
