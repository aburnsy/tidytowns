## Description

A formal multi-year plan demonstrates strategic thinking, continuity, and organisational maturity to adjudicators. **TMB's plan exists already — it is this website.** The public project tracker at `aburnsy.github.io/tidytowns/` is a complete, structured, multi-year roadmap of:

- everything we have completed (with photographs and costs),
- everything we are actively delivering (with status and benefit),
- and everything sitting in the future pile awaiting the right capacity or funding.

The site is updated continuously, links the work back to specific adjudicator recommendations, and is openly auditable by anyone — committee, residents, council, adjudicators.

## What is left to do

- **Reference this site explicitly in the 2026 application text** so the adjudicator reads our plan as exactly that — a living public document, not a static PDF.
- **Light tidy-up** before the entry deadline: ensure the *Projects*, *Completed*, and *Future* tabs are consistent and well written.
- **Optional:** generate a PDF snapshot of the active project list at submission time as an attachable artefact, so the plan is discoverable even if the adjudicator doesn't follow the link.
