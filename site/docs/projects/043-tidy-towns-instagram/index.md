## Description

A dedicated TidyTowns Instagram account would let us share project updates, before-and-after photos, and community events with the wider community. The committee agreed we'd be daft not to have one, builds public awareness, attracts volunteers, and is the kind of place you can drop a quick before-and-after of a planting day with no fuss.
