## Description

High-performing Tidy Towns villages consistently invest time in crafting clear, evidence-rich application text that tells a compelling story. Analysis of high-scoring application writing shows that the quality of the written submission can make a meaningful difference to how adjudicators assess a village's work. This project covers the writing-side improvements we are folding into the 2026 entry — beyond what individual delivery projects already record.

## Specific writing improvements for 2026

- **Feature the Bog Walk Loop** in the application (it was absent from the 2025 entry despite being one of our biggest amenity assets) — see [project 028](../028-bog-walk-nature-trail/index.md) for the full programme.
- **Connect the school's sensory garden to the Green Schools programme** — the 2025 adjudicator asked whether the school was using the sensory garden as part of Green Schools activities. Make this connection explicit in the Nature & Biodiversity narrative.
- **Map our projects to UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** — adjudicators have praised entries that explicitly reference the SDGs. Tagging projects to relevant SDGs (especially SDG 15 Life on Land, SDG 11 Sustainable Communities, SDG 17 Partnerships) demonstrates an internationally-aware approach with no extra delivery cost.
- **Lead with measurable evidence** — bulb counts, FIT count results (see [project 045](../045-laudato-si-returning-to-nature/index.md)), volunteer hours, before/after photographs.
- **Quote partner organisations by name** — Tipperary County Council Biodiversity Officer, Heritage Officer, Parish Pastoral Council, SMJ Construction (derelict refurbishment, see [project 014](../014-derelict-property-improvements/index.md)).
