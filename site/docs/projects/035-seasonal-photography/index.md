## Description

High-quality photo and video documentation across the year lets us demonstrate the full scope of the village's Tidy Towns effort in a compelling, visual way. Both exemplar villages Emly and Silvermines were praised by adjudicators for their photographic documentation, and Silvermines specifically for their social media video content.

Two strands run together:

- **Set-piece seasonal shoots** — spring, summer, autumn, and winter sweeps capturing the village at its peak in each season, used for the application photo set.
- **Ongoing rolling documentation** — photos and short video clips of project work, community events, and pollinator visits as they happen, captured by the volunteer team and dropped into a shared library each month.

This costs nothing but enriches both the application and our [Instagram presence](../043-tidy-towns-instagram/index.md).
