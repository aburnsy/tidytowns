The 2025 adjudicator asked us to do something about the look of derelict buildings around the village. The committee can't make property owners refurbish, and we don't want to claim credit for things that happen on private property independently of us. The honest position on this one is that a few good things have happened on the main street this year and we're delighted to see them.

SMJ Construction, run by **Seanie Mockler**, has been refurbishing two derelict properties on the main street, with new slate roofs, fresh render and new sash-style windows all in place. The planning was already submitted before any adjudicator commentary; this is private work on private property. We're commending it here because the clean aesthetic and the craftsmanship on display are genuinely a lift to the streetscape, and Seanie is a local fella we're very proud of, also a member of TMB Tidy Towns and the Development Group.

![Main-street property mid-refurbishment by SMJ Construction (May 2026)](main-street-refurb-may-2026.jpg)

The former Dempsey & Harold Motors site at the western end of the village closed in spring 2026 after decades as a working garage in the village. That closure is a sad loss in itself. The site has been taken on by Roadvacs (Irl) Limited as their new Tipperary base, with offices and a yard rather than a trucking operation. Excited by the prospect of new business activity at this end of the village.

![Former Dempsey & Harold Motors site, now being redeveloped by Roadvacs (May 2026)](former-dempsey-motors-may-2026.jpg)

The committee's own action on this adjudicator recommendation in 2026 was a formal report we submitted to Tipperary County Council on a derelict house opposite the cemetery on the L4202, where overgrowth was spilling onto the public footpath.

![Derelict house opposite the cemetery on the L4202 (May 2026)](derelict-property-cemetery-may-2026.jpg)

About as much as a community group can do on this one, and we will keep an eye on the rest.
