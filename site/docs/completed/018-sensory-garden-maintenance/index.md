## Description

The sensory garden's timber pathways and fixtures have deteriorated, with some sections impeding access and damaged features reducing the quality of the experience. A focused maintenance blitz to repair and treat all timber elements would restore the garden to full use and extend the life of the existing infrastructure.

## What the adjudicator said

The 2025 adjudicator noted that some pathway sections in the sensory garden were impeded and that several timber fixtures had been damaged, recommending a maintenance effort to bring the garden back to its intended standard.

## Progress (spring 2026)

The sensory garden has been substantially renewed: timber pergola repaired with locally sourced wood and a yellow-painted plinth, gravel paths re-laid, drystone amphitheatre rebuilt as a small outdoor classroom, carved wooden sculpture installed, and the entire boundary woven with living willow rods and natural stone alongside reclaimed timber. The garden now reads as a fully working sensory and learning space rather than a tired one.
