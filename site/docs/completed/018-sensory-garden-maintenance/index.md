The 2025 adjudicator flagged blocked paths and damaged timber. Spring 2026 we got stuck in: pergola repaired with locally sourced wood and a fresh yellow plinth, gravel paths re-laid, the drystone amphitheatre rebuilt as a small outdoor classroom, a carved wooden sculpture installed, and the boundary woven through with living willow alongside reclaimed timber. Reads as a proper sensory and learning space again.
