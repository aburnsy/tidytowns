**Patrick Hayes** designed and wove a living willow gateway for the sensory garden in March 2026. Volunteer craft, whips taken as cuttings, no cost. Living willow needs a light tidy each spring (tying in new growth, replacing the odd broken whip) and a full re-weave every two or three years to keep its shape. Next big re-weave pencilled in for late winter 2028 or early 2029.

Idea floated: run a short willow-weaving demo at the next re-weave so the skill stays in the village beyond a single volunteer.
