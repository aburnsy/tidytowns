## Description

In March 2026, TMB Tidy Towns volunteer **Patrick Hayes** designed and wove a living willow gateway feature for the sensory garden. It is a hand-crafted, no-cost addition that complements the timber maintenance work (Project 018) and brings a folk-craft element to the village's most-used contemplative space.

Living willow needs ongoing care to stay structurally sound and visually strong:

- **Light annual touch-up** — tying in new growth, trimming weathered ends, replacing any broken whips.
- **Full re-weave on a roughly 2–3 year cycle** — new whips woven through the structure to refresh and reinforce it as older material weathers.

This project file exists to make sure the cycle isn't forgotten. Without it, the gateway will degrade and the volunteer-craft story attached to it is lost.

## Notes

- Best time to cut and weave fresh whips is late winter / very early spring, before the willow breaks dormancy.
- Willow whips can be sourced free from cuttings of existing village willows or from neighbouring growers — no commercial purchase needed.
- Consider running a short willow-weaving demonstration / workshop alongside the next re-weave so the skill stays in the village beyond a single volunteer.
