Follow us at [@twomileborristidytowns](https://www.instagram.com/twomileborristidytowns/). The committee agreed we'd be daft not to have one, so we got it sorted. Builds public awareness, attracts volunteers, and is the kind of place you can drop a quick before-and-after of a planting day with no fuss.

The launch grid covers the things people most often ask us about: the 1900 monument, the Blackcastle, the Bog Walk biodiversity, the sensory garden, and the Christmas lights night.

Posting cadence is one a week on a Sunday evening, batched ahead of time so it doesn't become an extra job. Anyone with village photos worth sharing can pass them on to the committee for the queue.
