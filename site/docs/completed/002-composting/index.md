The compost bin sits in the sensory garden and is actively used by both the volunteer team and the maintenance workers, green waste from village planting work goes in, and the resulting compost goes back into the beds and planters. Closes the loop on our green waste.

The 2025 adjudicator recommended we introduce composting as part of broader sustainable-waste / circular-economy practice. The bin was already there, but we will make sure the 2026 application text calls it out clearly so it doesn't get missed again.
