The adjudicator asked for unmown sections on the approach roads to support pollinators. Done. Strips run along the inside of the verges and along the main road heading west into the monument. The native seed bank is doing the work, with naturalised allium and chamomile coming up through it.

Photos on the [village map](../../map.md) under monument, leighton manor, and seating area. We're following the [All-Ireland Pollinator Plan grasslands guidance](https://pollinators.ie/grasslands/).
