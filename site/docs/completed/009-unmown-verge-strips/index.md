The adjudicator asked for unmown sections on the approach roads to support pollinators. We have significant strips at the seating area, the N75 approach roads and the approach from the Turnpike outside Leighton Manor. The native seed bank is doing the work, with our added allium bulbs coming up through it.

We do have an outstanding question with the Tipperary County Council / Biodiversity Officer Ann-Marie concerning who has been mowing the verges on the N75.

We've also agreed not to mow the curving border at the Castlepark entrance corner, which sits under the mature Sycamore canopy. The bark mulch lets the native wildflowers come up naturally each spring from the seed bank, and the dappled shade from the Sycamore is exactly the conditions Cow Parsley, Dandelion and the rest are happiest in.
