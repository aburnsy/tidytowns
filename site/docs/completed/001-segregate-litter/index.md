The 2025 adjudicator flagged that everything collected on cleanups was going straight to landfill, and asked us to start separating out the recyclables at the point of collection.

Volunteers now recycle what bottles, cans and other clean recyclables we can. Unfortunately the vast majority of what gets collected is in no state to recycle.
