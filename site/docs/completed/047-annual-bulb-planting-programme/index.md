A big planting weekend in autumn 2025, TMB Tidy Towns volunteers joined by volunteers from the residential estates. **11,100 bulbs** went in across the village core, the monument, the sensory garden, the cemetery entrance, the estate entrances, and the N75 approach. The alliums carry the show through May to July, well past the daffodils and crocuses, and the bulbs naturalise so the display comes back stronger every spring.
