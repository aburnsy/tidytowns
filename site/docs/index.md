# Welcome to Two Mile Borris

Two Mile Borris (Buirios Leith) is a small village in northeast County Tipperary, about 7km east of Thurles and just off the M8. The village has a primary school, a church and a graveyard, and a tight-knit set of community groups behind everything that happens here.

## Our Tidy Towns committee

The Tidy Towns committee has 27 members and is supported by a wider pool of village volunteers. We work year-round on:

- cutting waste and improving recycling
- bringing back biodiversity in our verges, hedges and shared spaces
- lifting the streetscape, signage and approach roads
- getting more residents involved in the village

This site is our public project tracker. Every project we're working on, planning, or have completed is listed here so the village can see what's happening and step in where they can.

## Community groups

Two Mile Borris runs on volunteer effort. Alongside Tidy Towns, the village has:

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-home-group: __Development Group & Tidy Towns__

    Working with Tidy Towns to improve infrastructure and quality of life in the village.

- :material-school: __Parents Association__

    Fundraising for the primary school and supporting school activities.

- :material-string-lights: __Christmas Lights Committee__

    Plans and fundraises year-round to light up the village every December.

- :material-grave-stone: __Graveyard Committee__

    Maintains the graveyard and is delivering the new graveyard extension.

- :material-heart-pulse: __Defibrillator Group__

    Maintains the village defibrillators and runs emergency-response training.

</div>

For wider village news, fundraising, and the Split the Pot weekly draw, see [tmbvillage.ie](https://www.tmbvillage.ie/).
